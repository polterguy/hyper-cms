About the firm

Twelve people and one very good filing cabinet.

We have been quietly correcting other people's paperwork since 1963, from two rooms above a tailor's on Savile Row.

Our history

The firm was founded by Margaret Pemberton, who spent the war deciding which supply requisitions were real, and Arthur Vale, who spent it losing them on purpose. They disagreed for thirty years and were right about different things.

The practice has grown by four people since. We are told this is slow. We are also told, by the same people, that nothing has ever gone missing.

How we work

We do not ask

Whatever happened is between you and the committee. We need the dates, not the details.

We do not keep copies

One folder, handed over, and the drawer is empty again by Friday.

We do not hurry

Six working days. It has been six working days since 1963 and we see no reason to revisit it.

Finding us

There is no sign on the door. There is a bell, and it works, which distinguishes us from most of the street.

Enquiries are handled in person. We are told this is inconvenient. It is, and that is rather the point.
